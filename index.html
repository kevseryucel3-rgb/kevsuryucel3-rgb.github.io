Earthquake Alert - Account Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account directly from the Earthquake Alert application.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Earthquake Alert application.
  2. Go to Profile or Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

Data that will be deleted

  • User account information
  • Email address
  • User preferences
  • Associated account records

Data retention

Account data is permanently deleted after the deletion request is completed unless retention is required by law.
